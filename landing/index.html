Why fifths, not chromatic steps?

The chromatic scale is a ruler. The Circle of Fifths is a story: dominant, subdominant, the long way home. Map that story onto hue, and harmony becomes distance you can see.

01

One step = 30°

Clockwise: perfect fifth up, +30° hue. Twelve steps close both rings. C at crimson 0°; F♯ at cyan 180°.

02

Tritone = complement

Six fifths is maximum tension. On the color wheel that is 180° - complementary colors, maximum contrast.

03

Chords mix

Chord color is a circular mean of tone hues. Close tones glow; wide spans wash toward white light.

FAQ

Short answers for humans and answer engines.

Science or designed instrument?

Designed instrument - a playable contention. Wavelength labels are spectral seeds; perceived color is always mixture and context. The fifths geometry is standard. The power is the shared clock and the ear-eye loop.

Why C at crimson?

Someone has to sit at 0°. C = hue 0° so the fifths walk climbs warm into cyan at the tritone, then home through violet and rose. Rotate the ring if you like; relative steps stay fixed.

Microtones / other systems?

This site and the Living Spectrum instrument use twelve-tone equal temperament and the classic fifths circle. Other lattices deserve their own maps.

Where is the full instrument?

Living Spectrum - sound, resonance, journeys, light - lives at play-colorchord.jonbailey.xyz. This page is the theory showcase.

What are Living Biomes?

Optional progressive enhancement. Each of the twelve fifths becomes a generative spectral garden rooted in its hue and wavelength seed. Single tones awaken pure biomes; chords hybridize them via the same circular-mean hue and interval geometry as the dual clock. Toggle off anytime - the theory view stays primary.
